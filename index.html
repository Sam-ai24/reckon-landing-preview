What we recover

Idle cash, forgotten permissions, and stranded assets sitting across chains. The money is usually already yours.

  • Idle stables earning nothing USDC and USDT parked in operating wallets instead of a yield venue you already approved.
  • Open allowances on old contracts Unlimited approvals left behind after a migration, still able to move funds if those contracts ever change.
  • Failed swap dust Tokens left in routers and aggregators after a revert, too small for a dashboard to flag and large enough in sum to matter.
  • Assets stranded after a bridge Incomplete transfers, unused destination gas, and inventory sitting on a chain nobody on the team checks.
  • Unclaimed staking rewards Validator, restaking, and liquidity rewards that accrue quietly and expire or dilute if nobody claims them.
  • Gas left in unused deployers ETH, SOL, and stable gas reserves on old deployer keys, multisigs, and test wallets from the last three years.

How a scan works

Read-only first. Nothing moves without your signature.

  1. 01

    You share addresses

    Vaults, multisigs, and operating wallets. We never ask for a seed, a key, or custody.

  2. 02

    We map every leak

    Across Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Solana, and the bridges between them, then send you a ranked recovery list.

  3. 03

    You approve what returns

    Each recovery needs your signature. The first $250,000 stays in your wallets. After that, the 10% fee applies.
