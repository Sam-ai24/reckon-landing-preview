Four facts on every tag

Written down before you pay, never left for you to guess.

  • Cut

    Shape, cutting style, carat weight and size in millimetres. Diamonds also carry their colour and clarity grades.

  • Origin

    The country the stone was mined in, when a laboratory can tell. When it cannot, we say that too.

  • Treatment

    Heat, oil or nothing at all. Most rubies and sapphires are heated, and most emeralds are oiled.

  • Report

    Stones over one carat come with a report from an independent laboratory such as GIA, GRS or Gübelin.
